CHASING THE TRASH BOT
=====================

“Wait!" Hepi shouts.

Instead of waiting, Proti just shouts, “hurry! Trash bots empty quickly and often!”

Hepi runs after Proti who is running after the trash bot. It stops to collect the trash from a can at the edge of the food court. Hepi catches up and they see the reader. Proti glimpses the very edge of her hand-drawn decorations inside the reader. She reaches for the reader just as the bot dumps food-court trash all over it. She recoils from the stinky old food containers and whatever is dripping out of them.

They both stand there stunned and grossed out as the trash bot departs happily to empty itself at the mall dump. Proti jumps back into action and Hepi is close behind.

They do not try to stop the trash bot - with its strong motors. They pass it, planning to catch it as it turns into the maintenance corridor. They make it to the double doors to the corridor with two moments to spare. Before the trash bot reaches them, it detours into the old arcade which has been enclosed in a “coming soon!” construction wall since before Proti was old enough to get her own Mallowance. She has never spent her own money at the mall arcade.

Confused (maybe it’s going to pick up some construction debris?), the two friends follow the bot, ducking into the access door before closes. Good thing too, the door doesn’t even have a doorknob on the outside!

They see the robot blinking toward the door at the back of the old arcade. The trash bot doesn't pick up any of the construction debris. But it slows down as it steers around it.

They catch up, and the machine is moving slowly enough that they can actually sift through the trash while it is moving. It's almost (but not quite) completely dark in here. What night vision they've gained in the few moments here in the dark arcade is ruined every time one of the lights of the robot blink.  Proti pulls out her tiny flashlight. Its better than nothing, but it isn't very bright. And Proti has to hold the button. She chose that type of button so she could blink Morse code to Hepi. But Hepi never got a matching light.

The dim flashlight glints off of a shiny part of the card reader. Hepi yanks it out of the can. She wonders if she could just yank the whole can off of the bot base. She is glowing with triumph! She looks at the reader, and back up to Proti. She doesn't know what to say!

Proti says, "good job!"

Anybody would be surprised to get such praise from Proti. The triumph of finding the part multiplies with the praise and Hepi lets out a war cry!

The neon signs of the old arcade come on. There are lots of bathrooms where sound activates the lights. Sound-activated *normal* lights makes sense. Sound-activated neon signs make no sense. The two friends are hypnotized by the rhythmic buzzing of the animated neon signs.

Bizz, bazz, buzzz!
Dark.
Bizz, bazz, buzzz!
Dark.

"What now?" Hepi wonders to her friend.

Proti thinks out loud, "it was a trash bot, right? We saw it dump old pizza boxes on top of the card reader. They were going to throw it out for sure. I think we can keep it."

"But the card is *actually* yours," Hepi adds.

"Right but how do we get the card *out?*"

"I think we should take it home and try to open it up."

"Let's do it."
