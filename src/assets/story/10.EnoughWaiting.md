# 10: Enough Waiting

It seems like they have been waiting for an eternity. Hepi actually fell asleep.

It's been so long, Proti is so bored, that she decides to *do homework!*

She gets her MAGiE case out of her back pack. She opens the case and extracts her MAGiE. She turns on the power and the screen says 22:30! It **has** been forever! They went to the mall right after school! Her parents are going to be so mad!

She prods Hepi through the bars between their cells.

"Hepi! Wake up, it's twenty-two thirty!"

"What? That's ten thirty PM!"

"You said the key was probably just a card, right? We need to figure out what it might be. Do you have your empty Mallowance Card?"

"Oh, Sure," she hands the card through the bars.

"Thanks," Proti accepts the card and punches 'WRITE 2230' into her MAGiE, hits enter, and carefully swipes the card through the card slot of her MAGiE. It beeps a good beep and she stands up to try the card in the lock.

Even though they are maddeningly flimsy, Proti wanted to use the Mallowance cards in case the machine confiscated incorrect cards. She wanted to use Hepi's card first so if the card *was* lost or destroyed, she would still have her own. She notices how selfish that was and as a penance she tells Hepi, "thanks for letting me use your card for my experiment."

"Sure thing," Hepi says, very slightly stunned by the kind words. She noticed Proti pressing her head against the bars trying to see what she is doing. "Here," Hepi offers the compact mirror.

"Oh. Yeah," Proti mutters and then remembers to say, "thanks."

Being extra careful to get the fragile card lined up to go straight into the reader, Proti hears the little motor whine as it pulls the card in. Then she hears the bad beep and the matching whine of the card coming back out.

"Well, at least it didn't keep the card," she shrugs. Then she plucks the card out and is about to sit down.

"The screen." Hepi reminds her and glances at the lock box.

"Oh!" Proti holds the mirror out and wiggles it around, looking for the grey-green display.

It says:

BAD KEY 38567

Very confused.

"What does 38,567 have to do with 2,230?"

Hepi sounds discouraged, "oh, jeez - I don't know." She is more bored than discouraged; she does not find this "puzzle" interesting. But she is able to continue encouraging Proti as she tries a few guesses.

Proti cannot decipher the operation that got them from 2230 to 38567, so she counts up from 2230, writing 2231 to the card. Insert, beep, card ejects.

BAD KEY 38566

"Huh," Proti puzzles.

Write 2232, insert, beep, eject.

BAD KEY 38569

Proti is surprised. After a moment, she thinks out loud, "What the&#x2026; 2230 was 38567, 2231 was 38566. I was expecting 2232 to be 38565, but instead it was 38569."

"That makes no sense," Hepi agrees. "Try a few more."

After two more guesses, Hepi can't keep the results in her head and starts taking notes. Proti doesn't notice what Hepi is doing, but she is thinking there **is** some kind of pattern. She tells Hepi that, "2237 gave 'BAD KEY 38572'."

Hepi calls back, "got it."

"Got what?"

"Oh, I started writing them down."

"The bad keys?"

"Yeah - we thought they were just counting down as our guesses were counting up, right?"

"Right - they *do* count down, two in a row, then it jumps up by two."

"Three," Hepi corrects.

"What?"

"It jumps up three, not two."

"No, it's two, look," she tries to explain the mathematics to her ditzy friend. "sixty-seven, up two is sixty-nine, up two is seventy-one&#x2026;"

"Oh, I see - I meant it jumps up three from the actual number. It jumps up two from the **previous** previous number." Hepi is careful to phrase it in terms of what *she* was doing that didn't match what *Proti* was doing so Proti doesn't think that she is correcting her. "Sixty-six, plus three, sixty-nine. Sixty-eight, plus three, seventy plus three, seventy-three."

"Oh. Yeah." Proti sees that there was nothing wrong with what Hepi said and smooths it over with, "oh, yeah - we're *both* right!" This gives her a little warm fuzzy feeling and she smiles genuinely at her friend.

Hepi enjoys a warm-fuzzy of her own, but is not surprised by it, "Anyway - I started writing it down to keep track."

Hepi now understands what her friend meant, "oh, the biggest smaller number, why didn't you just say that?"

Proti would have punched her in the arm if they weren't in different jail cells. Perhaps that is for the best.

Hepi laughs at the idea of considerate security bots who locked them into a cage. Then she averts her gaze to give Proti some privacy. Then she realizes she could do with a bio-break as well. She finds the same facilities at the back of her cell and does the same thing. The jail toilet has a sink above the tank, so they rinse their hands, since there is no soap. Then they use hand sanitizer from their backpacks and drink from their water bottles. Figuring that the water in the sink *might* be clean enough to drink if they finish the water in their bottles.

Proti is exhausted. She lies down on the bed and sleeps.
