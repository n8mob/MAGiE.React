LEAVING THE ABANDONED ARCADE
============================

The two turn to go out the way they came in. They get to the door on the construction wall at the front of the arcade, but it is locked.

"Why is it locked?" Hepi ponders.

"We're not supposed to be here. This is robot territory." Proti thinks that's pretty obvious.

"But there isn't a doorknob on the outside, why would they lock the inside knob?"

"In case curious kids try to open it anyway. With a big suction cup or a crow bar or something," Proti shrugs.

"Okay, Sure. But then how did the trash bot open the door?"

Proti is puzzled, "I don't know." Then she remembers, "the opening door caught my eye, we were waiting for the bot to go to the maintenance corridor."

Hepi reasons, "if a person opened the door for the bot, they would have been here to stop us from following it in."

"Right. We were rushing to catch the door before it closed, I didn't think to look to figure out how it opened."

"Could it a pressure sensor? Like the automatic doors into the supermarket? Except, it only opens for robots?"

"I guess. But how could it tell a robot from a human? Most of the mall bots are bigger than school bots which are bigger than home bots. But mop bots are all pretty small. Even the big mall mop bots aren't very big. Do you think they weigh more than a big grown up?"

"No way: Mrs..."

"... Clamp," Proti finished along with Hepi. A frequent substitute teacher, Mrs. Clamp is the largest person either of them has ever seen in real life. Much larger than any mop bot. Even a mop bot with a trash can glued on top.

"What if the door would open for Mrs. Clamp? We've never seen her walk past the arcade construction wall. Maybe it *would* open. Does she weigh more than both of us put together?"

"Maybe. We're just kids."

"Speak for yourself, I'm a teenager!"

"*Soon-to-be...*"

Hepi loves banter and just laughs, "no way we weigh as much as her."

"Pairs of people walk by the construction wall all the time. I've *never* seen the door open."

The robot never stopped, even while they were sifting through the trash. And it just now reaches the back door of the old arcade. They notice the blinking lights of the robot acting up as it approaches the door. The idea formed in the mind of Proti, but the weird sound-activated timer on the neon lights has chosen this moment to turn off the neon. The sudden darkness knocks the idea from the head of Proti. But then her hypothesis is proved when the darkness reveals dark red answering blinks from a spot near the top of the door frame.

The look between the two friends confirms that they both noticed this and they both understand it. The pride of Proti causes her to believe that she had the idea *before* the door blinked answers to the robot and *before* Hepi had the idea. But she experiments with this "tact" thing, about which she has heard so much, by not sharing her "I thought of it first" theory with her friend.

Proti is still holding the blinky flashlight from *Signal Hut*. She flashes it toward the back door of the arcade. Nothing happens.

Proti hypothesizes, "Maybe the light is too small. Too dim from this far away."

The two girls take a step toward the back door.

Hepi lets out a single weird chirping sound. This worries Proti, "are you okay? Did you stub your toe?"

"No, I... actually yes, I stubbed my toe, but not bad. I stubbed my toe and started to shout in surprise, but then three ideas all happened at once. (1) The toe didn't hurt, I only barely stubbed it. (2) If I continue my shout, we can turn the neon back on and see to navigate. (3) If we turn on the neon, it might drown out the dim blinks from the door."

"That is some fast thinking, Hepi."

Again that weird feeling of hearing praise from Proti. Rather than get all mushy, Hepi points to her brain and confirms, "thinkos!"

Proti laughs and remembers why the two are friends. She thinks to herself that maintaining a friendship might actually be worth all the work you have to put into it.

Hepi asks, "where is that second flashlight? We can use one to see and the other to signal."

Proti hands the other light to Hepi and they navigate closer to the back door. She shines her light at the door frame and they both notice a little black circle near the top of the left side. Proti blinks the light twice and the circle blinks once. But the door does not open.

She blinks the light once and nothing happens. Once more and still nothing. She blinks it twice in a row, and the door blinks back once. Then Proti blinks once and the door blinks twice! But it still does not open.

Proti and Hepi ponder for a moment. We blink twice, it blinks once, we blink once, it blinks twice.

Hepi scowls in concentration, then shrugs, and blinks her light at the door twice and it answers once.

This irritates Proti. She was willing - happy even - to accept help from Hepi, but Proti wanted to solve the puzzle herself. She decides she would rather escape than be clever, so she decides to help Hepi concentrate by saying nothing.

Hepi answers: blink
The door blinks twice: blink, blink
Hepi: blink, blink
Door: blink
Hepi: blink
Door: nothing

Proti tries: blink, blink
Door: blink
Proti: blink
Door: blink, blink
Proti: blink, blink
Door: blink
Proti: blink, blink
Door: blink, blink, blink
Proti: blink, blink, blink

Proti pauses for a moment, and when the door does not blink, she quickly blinks a fourth blink.

Door: blink, blink, blink, blink, blink
Proti: blink, blink, blink, blink, blink, (no answer) blink

When they hear the click-buzz just like the doors of the apartments and the school, they don't even consider, "was that it? Did it work?" They simply reach for the door and pull it open.

The corridor is not dark, but the lights are too harsh and too dim at the same time. Robot tires have left tracks on the polished concrete. Hepi starts to follow the tracks.

"I think the other way is the way out."

Hepi justifies it as, "both ways look the same; let's just go this way."

"Robot tracks. That way is where all the robots are going."

"Yeah, it seems like a good bet!"

Proti is not so sure, but she follows Hepi.

Proti and Hepi have been around for a while now. They've seen a few malls. They knew that theirs was a pretty big one. But this is ridiculous. Inside the mall they must use architecture or design tricks or something to pull you into the stores or make them seem closer together. There's nothing like that back here. It makes the walk seem even longer than it is.

Hepi remarks, "this corridor is long."

"*Longos*," Proti agrees.

The robot tracks seem to get heavier every time they pass a door. A good indicator that all the robots *do* go this way. Proti finally notices that all the doors have store names on them. She feels dumb for being surprised by it. But this gives them a better idea of where they are.

The next door, marked, "Pretzel Barn," crashes open. They catch the heavenly scent of fresh-baked pretzels, as a trash bot comes out of the door. The tires of the bot squeak as it turns right and zips away from them. *Then* they catch the hellacious sweet-tang of garbage. The Pretzel Barn is on the outskirts of the food court.

Proti still votes for turning around, "I don't think we can get out that way unless we catch a ride on a garbage truck."

"Sounds fun!" Hepi giggles.

This "being a supportive friend" thing is going to be trouble.
